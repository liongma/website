This thing is so cool. I bought a diffraction grating and thought about how cool it would be if I could make something to decode the signals. I have it fed into a raspberry pi with a screen, that calibrates from LEDs and then uses that to find peaks at different optical frequencies. This is then used to find an element in its database to figure out what exactly is producing that light. Then, I used one of my many plasma generators to test how well it works. 

I got nm accuracy with this bad boy and it still works to this day!
