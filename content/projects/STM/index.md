This is a scanning tunneling microscope i made! If it looks janky, thats because it absolutely is. In fact, most times in the day, the thermal expansion of the device causes the SNR to be extremely low causing the device to be unusable. But for a couple hours of the day (for me around 4PM), the temperature peaks and theres a brief 20 minute window for some mesmerizing photos to be made.
