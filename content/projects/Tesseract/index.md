Continuing on with my theme of making glowing objects, the tesseract was something that really caught my eye. I designed my own stand and 3D casing for the device, hoping to cut sheets of acrylic with a solar film to create a very low cost 2-way mirror. I ended up making a better one a year later as a gift with added structural rigidity. The whole thing is programmed with an arduino and plays various mesmerizing patterns.
