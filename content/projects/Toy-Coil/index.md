Coils are super fun! I wanted to show other people the coolness of tesla coil power transfer. Besides, my large desktop tesla coils are a bit big, why not make one super small? This one uses the very popular slayer exciter circuit topology which requires very few components and does not rely on very precise tuning. The result is a pretty cute high voltage tesla coil that can still wireless transfer power but remains relatively safe.
